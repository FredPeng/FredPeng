Personalised "Products For You" section and better related products for WooCommerce, page-cache and Cloudflare friendly.

== Description ==
Learns which products are bought together and viewed together (item-to-item collaborative filtering with shrinkage), adds category affinity and trending products, and re-ranks for diversity. Use the shortcode [sophie_for_you] anywhere.
